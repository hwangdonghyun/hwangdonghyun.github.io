Beomyoung Kim, Chanyong Shin, Joonhyun Jeong, Hyungsik Jung, Se-Yun Lee, Sewhan Chun, **Dong-Hyun Hwang**, and Joonsang Yu

## Overview

ZIM extends zero-shot segmentation toward detailed image matting. The work introduces a process for converting segmentation annotations into matte labels, together with a hierarchical pixel decoder and prompt-aware attention. Evaluation uses MicroMat-3K and considers applications including inpainting and 3D reconstruction.
